Colour is the signal

Red is work, teal is rest, amber is get-ready, green is done. A dark fill drains down the screen as the interval runs, and the last two seconds flash in time with the beeps. You can call it from the far end of the gym without reading a number.

It calls the time out loud

"Work." "Rest." And on long intervals, the time itself — "thirty… one minute… one thirty" on the way up, "ten… five" on the way down. Ten people sharing a three minute plank, each with their own goal, none of them able to look at a screen.

Beeps go where your music goes

Announcements play through the same audio path as the beeps, so with a Bluetooth speaker connected everything comes out of the speaker — not half of it out of a phone in someone's pocket. Your music keeps playing underneath.

Installs like an app, runs like one

Add it to your home screen and it opens full-screen with no browser chrome. Every preset runs with no network at all, and a session that gets interrupted picks up where the clock actually is — not where it was when the screen went dark.

Pass a workout around with four characters

Share any workout and it gets a four-character code. Anyone types it into their own clock and the whole thing lands on their phone — every block, set and interval. No link to tap, no sign-up on their end, nothing to install first.

The code sits on screen while the workout runs and again when it finishes, so a class working off one clock can take the session home without anyone writing anything down.

Add it to your phone

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open the clock in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.

One thing worth knowing: iOS mutes web audio while the ringer switch is set to silent. Flip the switch off silent and the beeps come back.

Android

  1. Open the clock in Chrome.
  2. Open the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app.

Chrome usually offers to install it on its own after the first visit.

Pick one, hit start.

Tabata and a few other presets are already in there. Build your own when you want to.
